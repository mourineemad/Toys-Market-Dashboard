# 🧸 Toys Market — Sales & Profitability Analysis (Tableau)

An interactive retail analytics project built using **Tableau Public**, designed to evaluate sales trends, profit contributions, regional footprints, and store channel performance for the Maven Toys retail chain.

🔗 [View the live dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/views/ToysMarket/Cover?:language=en-US&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)

---

## 📌 Project Overview
The primary goal of this project is to provide retail stakeholders with an end-to-end operational and executive overview of product movement, margin health, and regional store performance.

### Key Objectives:
- Identify top-revenue retail branches and highest-demand SKUs.
- Analyze margin efficiency and profit distribution across product categories.
- Evaluate regional store distribution and geographic sales performance across Mexico.
- Provide itemized SKU-level unit economics and store footprint reporting.

## 🖥 Dashboard Architecture

### 1. Navigation & Cover Page
* Branded landing page featuring direct navigation to dedicated views: **Sales**, **Profit**, and **Reports**.

### 2. Sales Dashboard
* **Top 10 Stores by Sales:** Identifies flagship branches driving gross volume (e.g., Maven Toys Ciudad de México and Puebla branches).
* **Top 5 Products by Sales:** Pareto breakdown of core volume drivers (*Lego Bricks*, *Colorbuds*, *Magic Sand*, *Action Figure*, *Rubik's Cube*).
* **Geographic Sales Mapping:** Spatial visualization of sales distribution across municipal markets.

<img width="1650" height="1275" alt="Toys Market_page-0002" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4fff7747-8f4b-42da-b633-5fc59eb4e5d4" />

### 3. Profit Dashboard
* **Category Profit Margins:** Evaluates margin profitability across lines, highlighting high-margin categories (e.g., *Electronics* at 44.57% and *Games* at 30.27%).
* **Profit Contribution:** Measures share of gross profit generated by each category.
* **City by Profit (Map):** Pinpoints regional profitability and geographic margin variances.

### 4. Executive Reports
* **Products Report:** Granular SKU matrix breaking down Product Cost, Selling Price, Total Sales, and Gross Profit.
* **Stores Report:** Comprehensive operational table auditing store performance by location type (Downtown, Commercial, Residential).
